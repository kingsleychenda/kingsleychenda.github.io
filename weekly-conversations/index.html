Weekly Conversations

A weekly online meeting for people who can see that the model we live and do business by is coming apart, and who want to work out, with others who see it too, what comes next. The biggest difference perhaps is that this is not meant to go and gather dust on another hard drive, but is the actual foundational ground we can use to create our physical grounds, which we are currently identifying and securing. Clearly those types of discussions still have the opportunity to help refine the parameters of the places we are looking out for. Note: outside the US and EU. It may be one of the very rare places where you move from brainstorming to application.

Part of Metanoia: connected undertakings, all asking how fully developed a way of life we can imagine, and what it takes to get there.

Who It's For

People who really know what they're talking about. Hands-on experience, years of it. Most of them are outside academia and the sciences, or were thrown out. That doesn't mean they have nothing to say. On the contrary. Here they get a voice, and a coherent one.

CEOs who get what is happening. What we are living through is the collapse of a societal model. Even a very successful company cannot last when the conditions it was built on no longer exist.

Why I Started It

Most people who see this stay in their own corner, not knowing who to talk to.

There was a time when people from different fields thought together. Then science was cut up like confetti, and CEOs were parked in mastermind groups costing hundreds of thousands of dollars, open only to those who "qualified". Those who qualified were not necessarily the ones who understood what was going on.

This is the opposite: a place to meet and think across disciplines, with an open mind, curiosity, resources, experience and real questions, so ideas from different fields can cross-pollinate each other.

What Happens

Every week, the whole group meets online for a couple of hours. Each meeting is recorded.

The exchange is both intellectual and practical, in the sense of a systems view of life: think of F. Capra, G. Bateson, but not just them. Bring along, around a key subject, expertise, knowledge, experience, resources, questions, and your open and inquisitive mind. What can contribute to developing models as described in the manifesto: creating radically innovative ecosystems?

It is a safe place to talk about subjects whose gravity people realise but wouldn't dare raise elsewhere, if such a place even existed.

Between meetings, working groups take up the questions and enrich the reflections, discussions and plans for the whole group.

There is no Christmas break planned. In fact there is often more freedom from professional constraints over that period, to combine with family events.

You can bring a partner or a very close person who is interested too.

Examples of conversations: Breakdown / Breakthrough. Systems Thinking.

Not Another Archive

This is not one more collection of talks. It is a rare opportunity: awareness, experience, curiosity and discussion across specialities, held together by a developing systems view, the kind Fritjof Capra, Donella Meadows and Gregory Bateson wrote about.

And it has somewhere to go. What comes out of these conversations is the foundation for a new physical Metanoia hub, where people come together to test it and put it into practice. We are looking at summer 2027, not the next eon.

The Hub

A physical application ground, not a showroom. That means land where people learn by doing real experiments, physical learning and sharing work: building, crafts, growing food, water, energy. We may also start from an old building and build the rest together, with the support of experienced teachers and mentors around.

I like to think that teaching and gathering experience, sharing feedback whilst actually creating something together, is a seldom opportunity. If you know anyone who is interested in joining the summer 27 crews, pass this on to them.

Each crew has a resident leader, mentors who rotate through, and we become the "apprentices" who gather practical experience and dirty boots by doing on the grounds.

We can then make sure to record what we are doing, so it becomes the learning files for the next crews and the basis for analysis of what works and what does not, as well as why.

Those records are then also translated, and when they reach the Mtelos LLM they can invite comments and experiences from other people who were not necessarily on the ground at the time but have some solid experience to contribute.

The questions come first. We start from what others have already tried: what worked, what didn't, and why. The conversations here change the vision. Each one of us has a gem to bring to the build of this burgeoning bunch of people who will end up creating their own way of life together.

The reach is meant to go far beyond a small group of people engaged in some way or other, and those of us who have something extraordinary to contribute now have a starting point: the Metanoia weekly meetings.
