Weekly Conversations

Every week I bring together, online, people who can see that the way we live and do business is breaking down, and who want to work out with others what comes next. Then we put it into practice, in a place built for that.

This is one of the doors of Metanoia, a set of connected projects that all ask the same question: how fully developed a way of life can we imagine, and what would it take to get there?

Who It Is For

Two kinds of people, who rarely end up in the same room.

The first are people who really know what they are talking about, because they have done it, often for decades. Most of them are not in academia or the sciences, and some were pushed out. That does not mean they have nothing to say. Quite the opposite. Here they get a voice, and the chance to make it heard alongside others.

The second are CEOs who understand what is happening. What we are living through is the collapse of a societal model. A company can be doing very well today and still have no future, because the conditions it was built on are disappearing.

Why I Started It

Most people who see this are on their own. They do not know who to talk to, and they keep their thoughts to themselves.

It was not always like this. People from different fields used to think together. Then science was cut up like confetti, each discipline in its own box, and CEOs were sent to mastermind groups costing hundreds of thousands, open only to those who "qualified". Qualifying was about money and status, not about understanding what was going on.

These conversations are the opposite of that. People come from any discipline, with an open mind, real experience and real questions, so that what one field knows can feed another.

How It Works

The whole group meets online for a couple of hours every week. Each meeting is recorded for those who could not be there.

The exchange is both intellectual and practical: what people know, and what they have actually done. It is also a safe place to talk about the things people see clearly but do not dare raise anywhere else.

Between meetings, smaller working groups take a question further and bring back what they found.

There is no Christmas break. That is when people have time to think, so there are more meetings over Christmas and New Year, not fewer.

You can bring a partner or someone close to you who is interested in this too.

Two examples of what we talk about: Breakdown / Breakthrough, and Systems Thinking.

Where It Leads

This is not meant to become one more archive of talks. It is a rare chance to think together inside a systems view, the way Fritjof Capra, Donella Meadows and Gregory Bateson looked at life: as connected wholes, not separate parts.

And the thinking has somewhere to go. What comes out of these conversations becomes the basis for a new physical Metanoia hub: a place where people live and work together, learn by doing, and test what was discussed on real land, with real buildings, food, water and energy. Everything learnt there is recorded and passed on, so it reaches people who will never come.

We are looking at summer 2027, not the next eon.
